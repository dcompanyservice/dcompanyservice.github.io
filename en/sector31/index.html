Gameplay

Every stage is a grid. Plan each step, deal with obstacles, and reach the exit.

Each stage starts at the entrance of a grid field.

Move one tile at a time and find a safe path.

Sometimes the best path is through an obstacle.

Screenshots are from a development build and may change.

Features

  • Grid-based puzzle strategyShort stages where every move counts. Each stage comes with its own clear objective.
  • Characters with their own stylesEach race comes in bare-hand, hammer and bomb types, each with its own look and motion.
  • A journey across planetsEvery planet has its own environment and story waiting in its depths.
  • Play with othersRankings, friends and guilds are part of the adventure.

Races

Every race in this world is named after a kind of bread.

Toast

A race with roots on the planet once called Earth, carrying on its space-suit culture. Adapted to harsh space, their bodies themselves became survival gear.

Twisted Doughnut

Born on Twistera, a planet of twisted gravity. They sense unstable flows of energy and find the “weak veins” running through rock.

Hot Dog

A fire clan that survived Ignis, a planet of lava and black ash. They awaken the heat sleeping in rock — though the fire can hurt them too.

Planets

The journey begins on a peaceful planet — but not every planet stays that way.

Pasture

A planet with an environment similar to that of an ancient world called Earth, which existed in another dimension. It is peaceful.

Water

A planet covered in water. It feels peaceful, but gazing into its depths somehow brings a sense of fear.

Ice

A planet covered in ice. Stay a while and it starts to feel just a little cold.

Fire

Fires burn here and there across this planet. It does not look dangerous unless you get close — but it is hot.
