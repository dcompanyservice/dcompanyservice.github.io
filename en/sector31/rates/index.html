If the base is +9 or lower, the target is picked uniformly at random from base+1 to base+material level (+1 if the material is +0), up to +10, and that level's success rate applies. From +10, the base goes up by +1.

Enhancement rules

- The material is consumed whether the attempt succeeds or fails.

- "Base lost on fail" is the chance that the item being enhanced is also lost when the attempt fails.

- On success, power = average of the two powers − a value picked uniformly from 1 to 5 (souls/emblems round down to steps of 5, characters to 1). Range 10–100; if both are 100 (P), it stays 100.

- When a character enhancement to +11 or higher succeeds, stats that have received fewer than 2 bonuses are offered as 3 random candidates (uniform) and you pick one.
