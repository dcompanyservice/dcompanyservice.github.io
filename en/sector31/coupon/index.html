Sector31 Coupon

Sector31 · Dcompany

Enter a coupon and the reward is sent straight to your in-game mailbox. If you open this page from the Coupon button in the game settings, your Game ID is filled in for you.

In the game: Friends → "My Friend Code" (8 letters/numbers)

  • Each coupon can be used once per account.
  • Each coupon has its own period and quantity.
  • Unclaimed reward mail disappears after 30 days.
  • Coupons cannot be sold or exchanged for cash.
