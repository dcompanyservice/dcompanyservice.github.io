Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026 (last updated: October 9, 2026)

Dcompany ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information on this website (sector-31.com) and in our game Sector31.

For our memo app AllMemo, please see the separate AllMemo Privacy Policy.

Note: Sector31 is currently in development. This policy describes how the game is designed to handle information and will be reviewed and updated before the game is released.

1. Website

This website is a static information site. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not set our own cookies. On some pages, to understand how often they are read, your browser sends the address of the page to our server, and we only count the number of views per page per day. We do not store IP addresses, cookies, or device information for this count, and it cannot be used to identify you. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; as the hosting provider, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, in accordance with GitHub's own privacy statement.

If you email us, we use your email address and the contents of your message only to respond to you.

3. Advertising

Sector31 offers optional rewarded advertisements (Google AdMob). Whether to watch an ad is always your choice. Google AdMob may collect and process device advertising identifiers and related information under its own privacy policy. On iOS, we ask on first launch whether you allow app tracking; if you do not, ads are shown without using the advertising identifier (the reward is the same). To confirm that ad rewards were delivered correctly, we store the ad transaction ID and your account identifier sent by the advertising provider.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate our services:

  • App store platforms (Google, Apple) — for downloads, payments, purchase verification and, if you link your account, sign-in authentication
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — for hosting our game servers, databases and content delivery (such as Cloudflare)
  • Advertising providers (Google AdMob) — as described in section 3
  • Authorities — when required by applicable law

Other players can see information you make public in the game, such as your nickname, profile icon, rankings and messages you send to them.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your game data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service.

Account deletion: You can delete your Sector31 account at any time in the game (Menu → Settings → Delete account) or by contacting us (see Delete your Sector31 account). When you request deletion, your nickname is released immediately and game progress is stopped. If you sign in again within 7 days, your account is restored (you may need to choose a new nickname). After 7 days, your account and personal information are permanently deleted and cannot be restored. Remaining paid in-game currency is not refunded and is forfeited.

Records kept by law: The following records are kept separately from your other personal information for the periods below and then deleted:

Other records required by applicable laws in other countries may also be kept for the period those laws require.

How we delete: Electronic data is deleted using methods that prevent it from being restored.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and encrypted storage of account credentials on your device (in the app's private storage area). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us at the email address below. We may ask you for information such as your in-game nickname to confirm your account.

9. Children

Where the law requires the consent of a parent or legal guardian to process a child's personal information (for example, for children under 14 in Korea), we will obtain that consent or will not process the information. A parent or legal guardian may contact us to access, correct or delete their child's information. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.
