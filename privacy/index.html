Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 17, 2026

Dcompany ("we", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information on this website (sector-31.com) and in our game Sector31.

For our memo app AllMemo, please see the separate AllMemo Privacy Policy.

Note: Sector31 is currently in development. This policy describes how the game is designed to handle information and will be reviewed and updated before the game is released.

1. Website

This website is a static information site. We do not ask you to create an account, we do not use analytics tools, and we do not set our own cookies. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages; as the hosting provider, GitHub may process technical information such as IP addresses to deliver and secure the site, in accordance with GitHub's own privacy statement.

If you email us, we use your email address and the contents of your message only to respond to you.

3. Advertising

Sector31 may offer optional rewarded advertisements. If advertising is included, the advertising provider (for example, Google AdMob) may collect and process device advertising identifiers and related information under its own privacy policy. Where required, we will ask for your consent before personalized advertising is shown, and this policy will be updated to reflect the details.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate our services:

  • App store platforms (Google, Apple) — for downloads, payments and purchase verification
  • Cloud infrastructure providers — for hosting our game servers, databases and content delivery (such as Cloudflare)
  • Advertising providers — only if advertising is included, as described above
  • Authorities — when required by applicable law

Other players can see information you make public in the game, such as your nickname, profile icon, rankings and messages you send to them.

6. Retention and deletion

We keep your game data for as long as your account is active and as needed to provide the service.

Account deletion: You can request deletion of your Sector31 account at any time (see Delete your Sector31 account). After a request, your account enters a 30-day waiting period during which you can cancel the request. After 30 days, your account and associated game data are permanently deleted and cannot be restored, including progress and purchased items.

Records kept by law: Records required by applicable law, such as purchase and payment records, are kept separately for the period required by that law and then deleted. We may also keep limited records needed to prevent fraud or resolve disputes.

How we delete: Electronic data is deleted using methods that prevent it from being restored.

7. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections and secure storage of account credentials on your device. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. To make a request, contact us at the email address below. We may ask you for information such as your in-game nickname to confirm your account.

9. Children

Where the law requires the consent of a parent or legal guardian to process a child's personal information (for example, for children under 14 in Korea), we will obtain that consent or will not process the information. A parent or legal guardian may contact us to access, correct or delete their child's information. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information without appropriate consent, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it.

10. International transfers

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the one where you live. We take steps so that your information remains protected in accordance with this policy.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, including before the release of Sector31. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.
