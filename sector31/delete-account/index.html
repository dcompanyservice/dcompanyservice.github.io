Delete your Sector31 account

Sector31 · Developer: Dcompany

You can request deletion of your Sector31 account and the data associated with it at any time. You do not need to reinstall the game to make a request.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to contact@sector-31.com with the subject “Sector31 account deletion request”.
  2. Include your in-game nickname so that we can find your account. We may ask for additional information to confirm that the account belongs to you.
  3. We will confirm by email when your request has been received.

If an account deletion option is available in the game settings, you can also request deletion there.

What happens next

  • Your account enters a 30-day waiting period. During this period you can cancel the request by contacting us.
  • After 30 days, your account and associated data are permanently deleted.
  • Deleted accounts cannot be restored. Progress, items and in-game currency, including purchased items, will be lost.

Data that is deleted

  • Account identifiers and account transfer codes
  • Nickname and profile
  • Game progress, items, in-game currency, match and ranking records
  • Friend lists, guild membership and messages linked to your account

Data that may be kept

  • Purchase and payment records, kept for the period required by applicable law
  • Limited records needed to prevent fraud or to resolve disputes

Records that are kept are stored separately and deleted when the required period ends.

Before you delete

Store purchases (Google Play / App Store) are not refunded when an account is deleted. If you have a refund request, please contact the store or us before deleting your account.
